📖 Tambola Rules & How to Play

What is Tambola?

Tambola (also called Housie, Tombola, or Bingo 90) is a lottery-style game where each player holds a ticket with 15 random numbers. A caller draws numbers from 1–90 one at a time, and players mark the matching numbers on their tickets. The first player to complete a winning pattern shouts “Tambola!” to claim the prize.

The Ticket

  • Each ticket has 3 rows and 9 columns
  • Exactly 15 numbers on each ticket (5 per row)
  • Numbers 1–90, arranged in tens: col 1 = 1–9, col 2 = 10–19 … col 9 = 80–90
  • Every ticket is unique — no two tickets are identical
  • Numbers in a column are always sorted ascending
  • Empty cells have no numbers — each row has exactly 5 numbers
  • A player can hold any number of tickets
  • Use the Tickets page to generate and print them
🏆 Winning Patterns
Pattern What you need Usually played
Early Five Any 5 numbers marked on one ticket First prize of the game
Top Line All 5 numbers in the top row Row prizes
Middle Line All 5 numbers in the middle row Row prizes
Bottom Line All 5 numbers in the bottom row Row prizes
Corners The 4 corner numbers of the ticket Optional prize
Full House All 15 numbers marked Grand prize — game ends
🎮 Game Flow

Step by step

  • 1. Distribute tickets to all players
  • 2. Pick a caller and agree on prizes
  • 3. Open the game page and choose mode
  • 4. Call numbers one by one (or auto)
  • 5. Players mark numbers as they are called
  • 6. First to finish a pattern claims it
  • 7. Verify the ticket, award the prize, continue

Fair play rules

  • The caller's decision is final
  • Shout “Tambola!” clearly to claim
  • Claims must be verified before the next number
  • A missed claim cannot be claimed after 2 more numbers
  • Only one player can win each pattern
  • If two players claim at once, the caller re-verifies both tickets
💡 Host Tips
  • ▸ Display the board on a TV using fullscreen (F11) so everyone can follow
  • ▸ Test the voice before starting — English, Hindi and Telugu are available
  • ▸ Start with Early Five to warm up, then lines, and finish with Full House
  • ▸ Use printed tickets and tokens or just cross them off
  • ▸ The game auto-saves — refresh and continue exactly where you stopped
